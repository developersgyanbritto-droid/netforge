Logo

Three versions of the logo

The stacked version is the primary logo. When the space calls for a different shape, use one of the other two. All three are the same logo, arranged differently.

Primary logo (stacked)
Icon on top, name below. The first choice for packaging, shopping bags, posters, shop signs and square posts.

Icon
The heart on its own. For profile pictures, app icons, clothing labels, stickers and very small spaces.

Horizontal logo
Icon on the left, name on the right. For wide, short spaces such as website headers, Facebook covers, banners and email signatures.

Anatomy

Logo anatomy

Every part of the logo has a meaning. Don't remove, change or add anything.

  • Orange heart
    Love and care: the love parents put into every choice they make for their child.
  • The "M" inside the heart
    The first letter of Miiniilove. Its puffy, rounded shape recalls soft toys and comfy clothes.
  • Yellow and teal blobs
    Play, colour and fun. They always sit behind the heart, never in front.
  • Splash marks on each side
    The energy and excitement of little ones.
  • Hearts on the "i"s and inside the "o"
    Love built into the name. Each of the four "i"s always has a heart on top.

Voice

Brand voice

Miiniilove talks like a favourite aunt or uncle: warm, cheerful and simple. It speaks to parents, with their children in mind.

Warm

Care and love come first, price second.

"Soft toys for tiny hands"

Cheerful

Short sentences and playful words. Few emoji.

"New colours, new adventures!"

Trustworthy

State fabric, age and safety details clearly.

"100% cotton · Ages 2–3"
